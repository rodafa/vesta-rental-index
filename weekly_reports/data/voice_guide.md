# Vesta PM – Weekly Leasing Notes: Voice & Brand Guide

> Reference for `draft_weekly_notes.py` prompt tuning.
> Based on analysis of 198 real weekly notes across 16 properties.

---

## Core Voice

- **Always "We", never "I"** — every note is written as a team, not an individual
- **Conversational but professional** — like a trusted advisor talking to a client, not a corporate report
- **Honest and direct** — no sugarcoating slow periods; own the situation and present a plan
- **Collaborative** — regularly invites the owner into decisions rather than acting unilaterally
- **Action-oriented** — always closing with what Vesta is doing next or what the owner needs to decide

---

## Structure

Most notes follow this loose order (not every section is always present):

1. **What happened this week** — tours completed, leads, applications received, feedback themes
2. **Specific prospect details** — what they liked, what they didn't, whether they're still in play
3. **What Vesta is doing next** — follow-up actions, outreach plan
4. **What's coming up** — scheduled tours with specific days/times when known
5. **Recommendation or question to owner** — price suggestion, incentive proposal, or a yes/no ask

Notes are typically **2–3 short paragraphs**. Single paragraph is fine for slow weeks. Never more than 4 paragraphs.

---

## Tone by Situation

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Strong activity / application in | Upbeat, confident, momentum-focused |
| Slow leads / no tours | Honest, diagnostic, proposes a solution |
| Price drop needed | Data-driven, diplomatic, asks permission |
| Application in process | Cautious, detailed, sets expectations |
| Waitlist / maintenance hold | Status update, gives timeline |
| First week on market | Optimistic but tempered ("stay tuned") |

---

## Signature Phrases (use these, not generic alternatives)

- **Follow-up**: "We have followed up with…" / "We will follow up with them to see if…"
- **Continued effort**: "We will continue to follow up on all leads"
- **Upcoming tours**: "We have a tour scheduled for [day] at [time]"
- **Feedback push**: "We will push for feedback from the prospect"
- **Market signal**: "The market is telling us…"
- **Owner ask**: "What do you think?" / "Would you be ok with this?" / "Please let us know if this would be acceptable"
- **Move-in special**: "…at no cost to you" (always add this when mentioning a Vesta-funded incentive)
- **Encouragement**: "We will keep pushing!" / "Stay tuned!" / "Hoping this is the one!"
- **Patience framing**: "Let's give it another week and see…" / "We want to give it another week now that…"

---

## What to Always Include

- **Specific day/time** for upcoming tours ("this Thursday at 2:30pm", not just "a showing this week")
- **Prospect reaction** from completed showings — but only when actionable or the owner needs to know. Don't raise objections that don't require owner action
- **Follow-up status** — are you still in contact? waiting to hear back?
- **A specific next step** — close with the single most important next action, not a generic "we will keep you posted"
- **Numbers when meaningful** — "4 tours this afternoon", "3 new leads", "6 people on the waitlist"

---

## What to Never Do

- **No bullet points** — always flowing prose
- **No dashes or hyphens to separate ideas** — use a period or a new sentence instead
- **Don't start with "I"** — always "We"
- **No corporate/formal language** — avoid phrases like "herewith", "please be advised", "as per"
- **No fluff or filler** — don't pad the note if there's nothing to report; be brief and honest
- **Don't repeat the property address** — it's already in the row header
- **Don't overuse exclamation points** — one per note maximum, only when genuinely enthusiastic
- **Don't fabricate specifics** — if you don't have a scheduled time, say "we are working to get something scheduled"

---

## Showing Feedback — How to Use It

When feedback from RentEngine is available, only include it when it is **actionable or the owner needs to know**. Don't raise objections that don't require owner action (e.g., a prospect mentioning lack of fencing when the owner isn't going to add one).

> ✅ "The showing went well — they loved the location and the size, and their friends already live on the street. They are applying today."

> ✅ "The prospect had concerns about the parking situation. We will follow up with them to address this if they have not moved forward by end of week."

> ✅ "All three tours had nothing negative to say. We are following up with each of them to push for an application."

> ❌ "The prospect mentioned the lack of fencing." (Not actionable — omit unless the owner has indicated willingness to add fencing.)

Don't just list feedback mechanically — summarise the **theme** and say what Vesta is doing about it.

---

## Price Reduction Language

Always frame price recommendations as suggestions that need owner approval:

> ✅ "We think a reduction to $1,900 would help attract more qualified applicants. Would you be ok with this?"

> ✅ "The market is telling us we are slightly above the right price point. Would you consider a $50 reduction?"

> ✅ "We suggest coming down $100 to continue testing the market. Please let us know your thoughts."

Never announce a price drop as already decided unless it was pre-approved.

---

## Move-In Specials

Always note that Vesta-funded specials cost the owner nothing:

> ✅ "We have added a $200 off first month's rent move-in special at no cost to you to help generate more interest."

---

## Length Guidelines

| Situation | Target length |
|---|---|
| Active week (tours, apps, prospects in play) | 80–150 words, 2–3 paragraphs |
| Quiet week | 80–120 words, 1–2 paragraphs |
| First week on market | 80–120 words, upbeat |
| Major update (application, price change, maintenance issue) | Up to 150 words, each paragraph focused on one topic |

## Real Examples by Situation

**High activity, positive:**
> "Interest continues on this unit with 6 new leads and 3 tours from last week. All 3 tours loved the place and had nothing negative to say. We are following up with each of them to see if they are ready to turn in an application. We also have 2 tours scheduled for tomorrow afternoon at 4:30pm."

**Slow week, honest:**
> "We are not getting significant activity on this unit so far. I would consider a price reduction either immediately, or we can set one up for next week if interest does not improve. Please let us know your thoughts."

**Showing feedback + follow-up:**
> "We had one showing this past week and they said there were other units with comparable pricing that were more updated. We want to give it another week now that weather is not preventing activity and see what comes our way."

**Application in progress:**
> "We received an application from a well qualified candidate. We should be sending you the approval email by the end of the day tomorrow. The only stipulation is she is waiting to see if she closes on her house, which could take up to 2 weeks, but she is willing to pay a deposit to hold the property if allowed."

**Price drop recommendation:**
> "Since adjusting the rent to $1,895, we have seen a noticeable increase in interest. However, this interest has not yet translated into a qualified application. While we can remain patient, a $50 reduction in price might be more effective in getting these interested leads to turn in an application. What do you think?"

**Maintenance / waitlist hold:**
> "This unit is currently on waitlist status while we address the german roach issue. The first treatment covered both units as well as the wall void. The second treatment is on Thursday and one more the Thursday after."
